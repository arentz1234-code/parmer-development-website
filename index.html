PARMER
DEVELOPMENT

Residential · Commercial

Parmer Development was established in 2015 when Andrew Parmer decided to focus his efforts on transforming one of the best geographical, yet most underutilized, locations in Auburn, Alabama: what is becoming known as “Uptown” Auburn — the section of Auburn bound by N. College on the East, N. Donahue on the West, Bragg Avenue on the South, and Drake Avenue on the North.

Since 2015, Parmer has developed and re-developed over 107 lots in Auburn, 89 of which are in “Uptown”. Developments have included 533 Judd Cottages, Uptown-1 by PD, The Arbors on White, The Neloms by PD, Tiger Braggs on Sanders, and Breckenridge Court. Current projects include Phase 2 Richland Road Mixed-Use and multiple Bragg mixed-use sites.

Parmer’s unique approach to real estate development has become synonymous with Auburn’s urban infill and rapid revitalization.
